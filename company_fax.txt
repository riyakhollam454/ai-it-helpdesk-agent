Company IT Helpdesk FAQ

VPN:

Employees who cannot connect to the company VPN should first verify that their internet connection is working.

Restart the VPN client and retry.

If the problem continues, restart the computer.

If VPN access still fails, the issue should be escalated to the IT Helpdesk.

Password:

Employees should use the company password-reset process when they forget their password.

Never share a password with the helpdesk team.

If the password-reset process fails, create an IT support ticket.

Email:

For email synchronization problems, check the internet connection, restart the email application, and verify that the mailbox is not full.

Persistent synchronization problems should be escalated to IT.

Laptop:

For a slow laptop, restart the computer, close unnecessary applications, and verify available disk space.

If the problem continues, contact IT support.

Security:

Never share passwords, one-time passwords, authentication codes, or confidential company information in a support chat.

Tickets:

Create a support ticket when troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue or when the issue requires access to systems that the chatbot cannot safely access.
