Product Support Document

Wireless Keyboard:

The wireless keyboard uses a USB receiver.

If the keyboard is not detected, reconnect the receiver, replace the batteries if applicable, and reconnect the device.

USB-C Hub:

If a USB-C hub is not detected, disconnect and reconnect it, restart the computer, and test another USB-C port if available.

Laptop Stand:

The laptop stand is a physical accessory and does not require software installation.
